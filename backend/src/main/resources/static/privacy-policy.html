1. 适用范围

本政策适用于由 CCPS 应用运营方提供的 CCPS 网站、管理后台、业主或租户服务以及相关通知功能。若某项服务另有专门隐私说明，以该说明为准。

This policy applies to the CCPS website, administration portal, owner or tenant services, and related notification features operated by the CCPS application operator. A service-specific notice will prevail where one is provided.

2. 我们收集的资料

根据您使用的功能，我们可能处理以下资料：

  • 账户及联系资料，例如姓名、用户名、电子邮箱、电话号码和加密后的登录凭证；
  • 物业及租赁资料，例如物业、单位、业主或租户关系、租约、付款进度、维修、文件及通知记录；
  • 身份及签署资料，例如为签署、授权或合规流程提供的身份证明号码、签名和验证记录；
  • 财务及交易资料，例如账单、付款状态、银行参考编号、收据及用户主动上传的付款凭证；
  • 通知资料，例如通知偏好、WhatsApp 号码、模板消息内容、发送状态、接收状态及用户回复；
  • 技术资料，例如 IP 地址、浏览器或设备信息、访问时间、安全日志和错误记录。

Depending on the features you use, we may process account and contact details; property, tenancy, payment, maintenance, document and notification records; identity and signature data used for verification or authorisation; financial and transaction records; WhatsApp notification preferences, message content and delivery status; and technical data such as IP address, device information, access time, security logs and error records.

3. 我们如何使用资料

我们仅在提供和管理 CCPS 服务所需的范围内使用个人资料，包括：

  • 创建、验证和保护用户账户；
  • 管理物业、租赁、付款、维修、文件、授权和客户支持流程；
  • 按照用户选择或业务需要发送服务通知、付款提醒、租约提醒和状态更新；
  • 处理 WhatsApp 消息、送达状态和必要的用户回复；
  • 检测滥用、调查故障、维护安全及履行适用法律义务。

We use personal data to create and secure accounts; manage property, tenancy, payment, maintenance, document, authorisation and support workflows; send service notifications and reminders; process WhatsApp messages and delivery events; prevent abuse; troubleshoot; maintain security; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. WhatsApp 与 Meta 平台

当您选择通过 WhatsApp 接收通知，或通过 WhatsApp 与 CCPS 互动时，我们会将为发送、接收和记录该消息所必需的资料提供给 Meta Platforms, Inc. 及其关联服务。该等处理也受 WhatsApp 和 Meta 自身条款与隐私政策约束。

CCPS 不会仅因用户提供 WhatsApp 号码而发送与物业管理服务无关的营销信息。用户可以联系我们停止 WhatsApp 通知；停止通知不会影响依法或为履行合同所必需的记录保存。

If you opt to receive notifications or interact with CCPS through WhatsApp, data necessary to send, receive and record those messages is provided to Meta Platforms, Inc. and its related services. Such processing is also governed by WhatsApp’s and Meta’s own terms and privacy policies. CCPS does not use a WhatsApp number solely to send marketing unrelated to the property-management service. You may contact us to stop WhatsApp notifications.

5. 资料共享与披露

我们不会出售个人资料。我们只会在下列情况下共享必要资料：向经授权的物业管理人员、业主、租户或业务人员提供其履职所需的信息；委托云端托管、电子邮件、WhatsApp、文件储存或技术支持服务商处理资料；取得您的指示或同意；或为遵守法律、监管要求及保护用户和系统安全。

We do not sell personal data. We share only what is necessary with authorised property-management personnel, owners, tenants or business staff; service providers supporting hosting, email, WhatsApp, file storage or technical operations; parties you direct or authorise; and authorities or other recipients when required by law or necessary to protect users and system security.

6. 保存期限与跨境处理

我们会在提供服务、处理争议、维护审计记录及履行法律或合同义务所需的期间内保存资料。不同资料的期限可能不同；不再需要时，我们会删除、匿名化或以限制访问的方式封存。由于 Meta、云端托管或其他服务商可能在不同国家或地区处理资料，相关资料可能发生跨境传输；我们会采取适用法律要求的合理保障措施。

We retain data for as long as needed to provide services, resolve disputes, maintain audit records, and meet legal or contractual obligations. Retention periods vary by data type. When data is no longer required, it is deleted, anonymised or archived with restricted access. Meta, hosting and other providers may process data in other countries or regions; we apply reasonable safeguards required by applicable law.

7. 资料安全

我们采用与资料性质及风险相称的合理技术和组织措施，例如访问控制、凭证加密、传输保护、日志记录和权限分隔。任何互联网传输或储存方式均无法保证绝对安全；如发现可能影响您的安全事件，我们会按照适用法律采取处理和通知措施。

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the nature and risk of the data, including access controls, credential hashing, transmission protection, logging and separation of permissions. No internet transmission or storage method is completely secure. We will respond to and notify affected users of qualifying incidents as required by applicable law.

9. 未成年人

CCPS 的物业管理服务不面向未满 18 岁人士独立注册使用。若我们获知在缺乏适当授权的情况下收集了未成年人的个人资料，将采取合理措施予以删除或限制处理。

CCPS property-management services are not directed to persons under 18 for independent registration. If we learn that a minor’s personal data was collected without appropriate authorisation, we will take reasonable steps to delete or restrict it.

We may update this policy when our services, technology or legal obligations change. The revised version will be posted here with a new effective date. Material changes will be communicated through CCPS or another appropriate method when reasonably practicable.
