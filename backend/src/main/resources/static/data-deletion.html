用户资料删除说明
User Data Deletion

您可以要求删除与 CCPS 账户及 Meta／WhatsApp 服务有关的个人资料。以下说明提交方式、身份验证、处理范围和处理期限。

You may request deletion of personal data associated with your CCPS account and Meta or WhatsApp services. This page explains how to submit a request, how identity is verified, what is deleted, and when the request is completed.

1. 提交删除申请

请发送电子邮件至 qq2290715152@gmail.com，主题填写“CCPS Data Deletion Request”。请提供与 CCPS 账户关联的姓名，以及电话号码或电子邮箱中的至少一项，方便我们识别相关账户。

Email qq2290715152@gmail.com with the subject “CCPS Data Deletion Request.” Include the name associated with your CCPS account and at least one associated phone number or email address so that we can identify the account.

2. 身份验证

为了防止未经授权的删除，我们可能要求您通过已登记的联系方式确认申请，或提供仅为验证账户所有权所必需的补充资料。验证资料不会用于其他目的。

To prevent unauthorised deletion, we may ask you to confirm the request through a registered contact method or provide limited additional information necessary to verify account ownership. Verification information is not used for another purpose.

3. 删除范围

完成验证后，我们将删除或匿名化在 CCPS 中与申请人相关且不再需要保留的个人资料，包括适用的账户联系资料、通知偏好、WhatsApp 授权记录、消息投递记录以及从 Meta 平台取得并由 CCPS 保存的相关资料。

After verification, we will delete or anonymise personal data associated with the requester that is no longer required, including applicable account contact details, notification preferences, WhatsApp opt-in records, message-delivery records, and related data received from Meta and retained by CCPS.

4. 依法保留的资料

法律、会计、税务、合同履行、争议处理、防止欺诈或安全审计要求我们继续保存的部分资料，可能无法立即删除。此类资料将限制访问和用途，并在法定或必要保存期限届满后删除或匿名化。

Some records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, tax, contractual, dispute-resolution, fraud-prevention, or security-audit purposes. Access to and use of such records will be restricted, and the records will be deleted or anonymised after the applicable retention period expires.

5. 处理期限

我们通常会在完成身份验证后的 30 天内处理删除申请。如适用法律规定不同期限或申请情况复杂，我们会告知预计完成时间。

We normally process a deletion request within 30 days after identity verification. If applicable law requires a different period or the request is unusually complex, we will inform you of the expected completion date.

6. 联系我们

如对资料删除流程或申请状态有疑问，请联系 CCPS 应用运营方：

qq2290715152@gmail.com

For questions about the deletion process or the status of a request, contact the CCPS application operator at the email address above.
